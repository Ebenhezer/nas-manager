Export

Downloads users, groups, roles, Samba shares, remote connections, sync and backup jobs, shared media libraries, alert rules and channels, upload quotas and settings as JSON. Without a passphrase, no secrets are included (password hashes, credentials, channel secrets and backup passphrases are left out). With a passphrase they are encrypted and the file is protected against modification.

Every import can be rolled back below.
